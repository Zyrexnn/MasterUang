### **The Bloomberg of Individual Wealth**

**MasterUang** is not just a tracker; it is an elite financial ecosystem and intelligence terminal designed for high-conviction decision making. Inspired by the Bloomberg Professional experience, it merges high-fidelity asset management with real-time global telemetry—tracking everything from your personal liquidity to the global distribution of airborne and maritime assets.

## 📡 Core Intelligence Modules

### 1. 📊 Sovereign Wealth Management
*   **Precision Engineering**: High-frequency logging of income and expenditures with intelligent categorization.
*   **Deep Analytics**: Interactive multi-dimensional visualization via **ApexCharts**, uncovering hidden patterns in your cash flow.
*   **Resilient Ledger**: Enterprise-grade data persistence powered by **Supabase** with bulletproof Row-Level Security (RLS).

### 2. 📉 Global Market Terminal
*   **Real-time Indices**: Instantaneous telemetry from the S&P 500, Dow Jones, and the Indonesian **IHSG (IDX)**.
*   **Asset Surveillance**: Live price streams for institutional commodities (Gold, Silver) and blue-chip equities.
*   **Crypto Command Layer**: A dedicated sub-terminal for high-volatility cryptocurrency price action and historical regression.
*   **Institutional Newsfeed**: Low-latency global financial news stream to maintain an informational advantage.

### 3. 🤖 AI Intelligence (Gemini Core)
*   **Contextual Synthesis**: Leverages the **Google Gemini Pro** LLM to analyze historical spending and provide surgical financial interventions.
*   **Autonomous Strategy**: AI-driven cash flow optimization based on real-time market conditions.
*   **Zen Interface**: A minimalist, zero-latency chat interface for executing complex financial queries.

### 4. 🛰️ Aerial & Maritime Telemetry
*   **Global Flight Logistics**: Real-time integration with the **OpenSky Network** for global aircraft surveillance, including altitude, velocity, and squawk telemetry.
*   **Maritime Intelligence**: AIS data integration for global vessel tracking and traffic analysis.
*   **Hyper-Optimized Rendering**: Custom-built viewport culling and spatial clustering to render thousands of assets smoothly on a single map.
